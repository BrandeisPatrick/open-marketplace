# Code of conduct

Open Marketplace adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. The Covenant is the authoritative text; this page summarizes how it applies here.

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about code and ideas, not about people.
- Assume good intent, ask questions when something is unclear, and help newcomers find their way.
- Take responsibility for your mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention or advances.
- Trolling, deliberately disruptive behavior, or sustained off-topic comments.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces, including the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and when someone represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Depending on the situation, responses range from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban from the project, following the enforcement guidelines in the Covenant. Comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this code of conduct may be edited or removed.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer, [BrandeisPatrick](https://github.com/BrandeisPatrick), privately through GitHub. Please do not report conduct issues in public threads. All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the person reporting will be respected.
